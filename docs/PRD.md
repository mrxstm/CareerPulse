Introduction
CareerPulse is an AI-powered career intelligence platform that helps job seekers evaluate their readiness for a specific role by analyzing their resume against a job description. Rather than relying solely on keyword matching, it identifies evidence-backed strengths, missing skills, and learning opportunities, then generates a personalized roadmap to improve career readiness.

Problem Statement
Existing resume analyzers often rely on keyword matching and provide arbitrary compatibility percentages without explaining the reasoning behind their evaluation. Users are left knowing what is missing but not how to improve. CareerPulse addresses this by providing evidence-based skill analysis and personalized learning roadmaps.
